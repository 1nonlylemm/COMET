# Appendix 2: Distribution Receipt for Learning Materials (Monitoring Template #1A / MT-1A)

**Appendix 2 (MT-1A)** is the official DILG institutional distribution receipt prescribed under the **Omnibus Guidelines** and referenced in the **PDMU Operations Manual** (Step 3 p. 17; Step 4 p. 19; Annex B Table 7). It serves as the formal institutional delivery and distribution receipt acknowledging receipt of learning materials from the Implementing Local Government Unit (LGU) to recipient educational institutions (e.g., public elementary schools, high schools, child development centers, or technical institutes) in good condition and in the specified quantities.

---

## 1. Official Title, Purpose, and Regulatory Scope

* **Official Form Title:** `DISTRIBUTION RECEIPT FOR LEARNING MATERIALS` (Internal Form Identifier: `MT#1A` / `MT-1A`).
* **Source Archive:** `others/reference_data/COMET Files, Forms, Annexes, and More!/Omnibus Guidelines Forms Annexes/Appendix-2-MT1A-Distribution-Receipt-for-Learning-Materials-8.10.22.xlsx` (Standardized August 10, 2022).
* **Catalog Titling Reconciliation:**
  * In the file system and form tab, this template is designated as `Appendix-2-MT1A-Distribution-Receipt-for-Learning-Materials-8.10.22.xlsx` and `MT#1A`.
  * In **Annex B (Table 7)**, it is listed as `Monitoring Template #1A (Description of Learning Materials)`.
  * In the document body (Row 4), the template banner carries the general heading `SUMMARY OF DISTRIBUTION FOR LEARNING MATERIALS` (a typographical carryover from MT-1), but the functional text and certification clause at Row 31 definitively establish it as a **Distribution and Property Acknowledgment Receipt**.
* **Statutory & Evidentiary Purpose:**
  * Establishes formal institutional receipt and acknowledgment of delivered goods by the beneficiary school or educational facility.
  * Serves as the mandatory primary evidence required by **Footnote 1 of Appendix 1 (MT-1)** to justify any quantities reported under the `THIS PERIOD` distribution column.
  * Aligns with Commission on Audit (**COA Circular No. 2012-001**) principles requiring formal acknowledgment receipts for government-funded property and goods distributed to recipient institutions.

## 2. Header Block and Recipient Institutional Metadata

The form requires dual-tier identification metadata: first identifying the funding LGU, followed by granular contact details for the recipient educational institution:

### Tier 1: Implementing LGU & Funding Profile (Rows 7–12)
* **`IMPLEMENTING LGU:`** Classification level of the source local government (Province, City, Municipality).
* **`NAME OF IMPLEMENTING LGU:`** Official name of the municipal/city government providing the goods.
* **`REGION:`** DILG Administrative Region (e.g., Region IV-A).
* **`PROJECT TITLE:`** Approved name of the education assistance or learning materials project.
* **`TOTAL ALLOCATION:`** Approved project grant budget in $\text{PHP}$.
* **`FUNDING SOURCE:`** Governing budget authorization (e.g., LGSF-FALGU, LBC 155, DRRAP).

### Tier 2: Recipient Institution Demographics (Rows 14–17)
* **`NAME OF INSTITUTION:`** Specific name of the recipient school or facility (e.g., *Santa Cruz Central Elementary School* or *Laguna Senior High School*).
* **`CONTACT ADDRESS:`** Exact physical street and barangay address of the school.
* **`EMAIL ADDRESS:`** Official DepEd or institutional email address of the school or administrator.
* **`CONTACT NUMBER:`** Active landline or mobile contact number of the institutional supply office.

## 3. Itemized Receipt Matrix Architecture

The body of MT-1A (Rows 19–30) contains a five-column schedule specifying the exact inventory delivered to the institution:

| Column | Header Label | Cell Coordinates | Description and Constraints |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **1** | **`NO.`** | Column A | Sequential line item number (standard rows 1 to 10 provided in template). |
| **2** | **`DESCRIPTION OF ITEM`** | Columns B–D (Merged) | Exact technical description and specifications of delivered learning materials (e.g., *DepEd Self-Learning Modules Grade 4 Science*, *7-inch Learning Tablet w/ Protective Case*). |
| **3** | **`QTY`** | Column E | Actual verified physical quantity delivered to and accepted by the institution. |
| **4** | **`UNIT COST`** | Column F | Approved procurement unit price per item in $\text{PHP}$. |
| **5** | **`TOTAL AMOUNT`** | Column G | Line item financial total ($\text{Qty} \times \text{Unit Cost}$). |
| **—** | **`TOTAL`** | Row 30 (Cols A–F merged; Col G sum) | Grand total monetary value of all goods transferred under this specific receipt. |

> [!NOTE] Absence of Embedded Formulas in Inspected Template
> The primary Excel file (`Appendix-2-MT1A-Distribution-Receipt-for-Learning-Materials-8.10.22.xlsx`, sheet `MT#1A`) contains **0 embedded formulas** or automated calculations in its blank state. Line item products ($\text{Qty} \times \text{Unit Cost}$) and column totals are unpopulated cells in the inspected template.

## 4. Certification Clause and Signatory Chain

Row 31 (cell A31) contains the explicit receipt and condition certification clause:

> *"We, the undersigned certify that the above items have been received in good condition and  in accordance in the quantity stated above. "*

### Institutional Signatory Hierarchy (Rows 33–39)

```
(NAME & SIGNATURE)
(SUPPLY OFFICER OF THE INSTITUTION OR EQUIVALENT)    DATE RECEIVED: ____________________


NOTED:

(NAME & SIGNATURE)
HEAD OF THE INSTITUTION
```

1. **`SUPPLY OFFICER OF THE INSTITUTION OR EQUIVALENT` (Rows 33–34):**
   * Printed on cell A34 as `(SUPPLY OFFICER OF THE INSTITUTION OR EQUIVALENT)`. Signs the receipt certification alongside the printed line `DATE RECEIVED: ____________________` (cell F34).
2. **`HEAD OF THE INSTITUTION` (Rows 36–39):**
   * Printed on cell A39 as `HEAD OF THE INSTITUTION`, under the heading `NOTED:` (cell A36).

## 5. Procedural Linkages and Evidentiary Boundaries

* **Subordinate Attachment to Appendix 1 (MT-1):**
  * Appendix 2 (MT-1A) does *not* track macro project balances; it is a **transactional delivery document**.
  * When an LGU claims in **[Appendix 1 (MT-1)](file:///c:/Users/marka/Projects/COMET/backend/app/brain/reference/appendix_01_mt1_learning_materials.md)** that $5,000$ reading modules were distributed "THIS PERIOD", the LGU must attach individual MT-1A receipts for every school that received batches of those modules, with the quantities across all MT-1As summing exactly to $5,000$.
* **Prerequisite for Beneficiary Distribution (MT-1B):**
  * Once the school supply officer signs MT-1A certifying receipt of the goods in good condition, the school proceeds to distribute items to individual students, logging each recipient on **[Appendix 3 (MT-1B)](file:///c:/Users/marka/Projects/COMET/backend/app/brain/reference/appendix_03_mt1b_beneficiary_report.md)**.
* **Role in PDMU Field Verification:**
  * During field monitoring (PDMU Operations Manual p. 17), DILG field officers visit recipient schools to perform physical inventory spot-checks against the quantities signed for on MT-1A.
* **Role in Project Closeout:**
  * In the completion stage (PDMU Operations Manual p. 19), full sets of MT-1A institutional receipts must be archived in the project dossier prior to final liquidation endorsement.

## 6. Practical Questions the Smart Assistant Should Answer

* **What is Appendix 2 / MT-1A?**  
  *Answer:* Appendix 2 (Monitoring Template 1A or MT-1A) is the official DILG Distribution Receipt for Learning Materials. It is a formal receipt used when an Implementing LGU transfers procured learning materials to a recipient school or educational institution.
* **Who signs Appendix 2 (MT-1A)?**  
  *Answer:* Appendix 2 is signed by the **Supply Officer of the Institution (or equivalent)** along with the date received, and is noted by the **Head of the Institution** (School Principal or Administrator).
* **What certification statement appears on Appendix 2?**  
  *Answer:* Row 31 states: *"We, the undersigned certify that the above items have been received in good condition and in accordance in the quantity stated above."*
* **How does Appendix 2 relate to Appendix 1 and Appendix 3?**  
  *Answer:* Appendix 2 (MT-1A) represents the institutional handoff from LGU to the school, providing the mandatory documentary evidence required to support the current distribution reported in Appendix 1 (MT-1). Once received under MT-1A, the school distributes the items to individual students, who sign the beneficiary roster in Appendix 3 (MT-1B).
