# Annex B: Documentary Requirements for Locally Funded Projects

This reference guide provides the complete, authoritative inventory of documentary requirements codified in **DILG Annex B (updated as of June 27, 2023)**, cross-referenced with the **PDMU Operations Manual Final (2024)** and {app_name}'s database models.

---

## 1. Statutory Identification & Legal Framework

* **Official Document Title:** Annex B — Documentary Requirements (updated as of June 27, 2023).
* **Governing Authority:** Level 1 (Official Authoritative Policy). Codified under DILG Memorandum Circular No. 2023-063 and corresponding Local Budget Circulars (LBC 131, 142, 146, 155).
* **Core Mandate:** Establishes the mandatory pre-requisites and implementation documents that Local Government Units (LGUs) must submit to DBM for fund allocation and to DILG PDMU for technical monitoring, evaluation, and regional endorsement.
* **Scope & Applicability:** Applies to local infrastructure, motor vehicle acquisition, and social/non-infrastructure assistance funded under the Local Government Support Fund (LGSF), including FALGU, DRRAP, Assistance to Cities (AC), and Growth Equity Fund (GEF).

---

## 2. Structure of Annex B

Annex B is structured into two distinct operational parts:
1. **Part I: Documentary Requirements for Fund Release (as submitted to DBM)** — Governs the initial project proposal, letter requests, sworn statements, and portal registration required before funds are credited.
2. **Part II: Documentary Requirements During Project Implementation (Monitored by DILG PDMU)** — Codified in Table 7 of Annex B, detailing the 30+ engineering, statutory, procurement, and progress documents tracked throughout project execution.

---

## 3. Part I: Requirements for Fund Release by Program & Fiscal Year

The documentary requirements required for fund release vary according to funding program and fiscal year issuance:

### Table 0: FY 2021 LGSF-FALGU
* **Vehicles:**
  * Signed Letter Request signed by the Local Chief Executive (Mayor/Governor).
  * Omnibus Sworn Statement (Annex B3).
  * Project Profile (Annex B4).
  * Inventory of Motor Vehicles (Annex B5).
  * Signed LGU Contact Details by the LCE (Annex B2).
  * Report on Fund Utilization and Program/Project Implementation (FY 2016–2020 LGSF-FA to LGUs), if applicable.
  * Certification attesting that balances from FYs 2016–2019 LGSF-FA to LGUs have already been returned to the National Treasury, if applicable.
* **Street Lighting / Barangay Electrification:**
  * Signed Letter Request.
  * Omnibus Sworn Statement (Annex B6).
  * Signed LGU Contact Details by the LCE (Annex B2).
  * Fund Utilization Report (FY 2016–2020), if applicable.
  * Certification on reversion of unexpended balances (FY 2016–2019).
* **Non-Infrastructure (Medical Equipment & Financial Assistance):**
  * Signed Letter Request.
  * Omnibus Sworn Statement (Annex B3).
  * Project Profile / Proposal (Annex B7).
  * Signed LGU Contact Details (Annex B2).
  * Fund Utilization Report and Treasury Reversion Certification.

### Table 1: FY 2021 LGSF-DRRAP (Disaster Rehabilitation and Reconstruction)
* **Covered Sectors:** Shelter and Resettlement; Public Infrastructure (roads, bridges, flood control, evacuation centers); Education and Health services; Agriculture; Social Protection.
* **Fund Release Prerequisite:** **None.** Funds were directly downloaded to beneficiary LGUs following the release of the covering Local Budget Circular.

### Table 2: FY 2021 LGSF-AC (Assistance to Cities)
* **Covered Projects:** Bicycle lanes, light mobility, motorized transport, pedestrian walkways, public parks/plazas.
* **Requirements:**
  * Signed Letter Request.
  * Project Brief signed by the LCE (Annex C).
  * Omnibus Sworn Statement (Annex D).
  * Report on Fund Utilization and Project Implementation (FY 2018–2020 LGSF-AC), if applicable.

### Table 3: FY 2022 LGSF-FALGU
* **Infrastructure, Medical/Rescue Equipment, Street Lighting, Non-Infra Assistance:**
  * Special Budget Request (SBR).
  * Report on Fund Utilization and Implementation (FY 2016–2021 LGSF-FA to LGUs).
  * Certification attesting that balances from FY 2016–2020 have been returned to the National Treasury.
* **Motor Vehicles (Ambulance, Trucks, Mini Dump Trucks, Multi-cabs):**
  * Special Budget Request (SBR).
  * Project Profile of Motor Vehicle.
  * Inventory of Motor Vehicles.
  * Fund Utilization Report (FY 2016–2021) and Treasury Reversion Certification.

### Table 4: FY 2022 LGSF-GEF (Growth Equity Fund)
* **Covered Projects:** All PPAs devolved to LGUs pursuant to Section 17 of RA 7160 (Local Government Code).
* **Mandatory Condition:** Projects must be included in the approved Devolution Transition Plan (DTP) of the LGU on or before the issuance of LBC 146.
* **Requirements:** **None.** Funds were directly downloaded to eligible 4th to 6th class municipalities.

### Table 5: FY 2023 LGSF-FALGU (Digital Portal Transition)
* **Covered Projects:** Agriculture facilities, farm-to-market roads, ICT systems and digitalization (IBPLS), public parks, active mobility bicycle lanes, disaster equipment, school sites, ambulances, and financial assistance.
* **Digital Submission Mandate:** Requests for financial assistance must be submitted strictly through the **Digital Requests Submission for Local Government Support Fund (DRSL)** in the DBM Apps Portal.
* **Prerequisites:**
  * LGU User Registration Form (Annex B) submitted to DBM Regional Offices for portal account provisioning.
  * Once the request is tagged as **"Approved"** in DRSL, the LGU submits to DILG:
    1. Annex C (Report on Fund Utilization for previous releases).
    2. Annex D (Project Profile/Proposal) for Assistance to Indigent Individuals.

### Table 6: FY 2023 LGSF-GEF
* *Source Status Note:* As of the June 27, 2023 Annex B update, the covering Local Budget Circular had not yet been released by DBCC/DBM.

Table 7 defines the documentary catalog monitored by DILG PDMU engineers and field officers during active execution. Requirements are partitioned by project modality:

### 4.1 Infrastructure & Electrification Projects (33 Discrete Items)

| Item # | Documentary Requirement | Statutory / Review Gate | Conditions & Notes |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Notice of Authority to Debit Account Issued (NADAI)** | Pre-Procurement | Official banking notice from BTr via DILG RO. Must record NADAI Number. Receipt triggers 15-working-day SubayBAYAN enrollment. |
| **2** | **Confirmation on Receipt of Fund** | Pre-Procurement | Official LGU acknowledgment of fund credit. |
| **3** | **Proof of Transfer of Fund to LGU Trust Fund** | Pre-Procurement | Bank certification/deposit slip establishing dedicated trust liability account. |
| **4** | **Approved LDIP, AIP, and DTP** | Pre-Procurement | Excerpt pages reflecting project appropriation and devolution compliance. |
| **5** | **Detailed Engineering Design (DED)** | Pre-Procurement | Complete architectural, structural, sanitary, and electrical drawings and specifications. |
| **6** | **Program of Works (POW) & Detailed Estimates** | Pre-Procurement | Itemized physical quantities, materials, equipment schedules, and budget ceiling approved by Mayor. |
| **7** | **Detailed Unit Price Analysis (DUPA)** | Pre-Procurement | Unit cost breakdown evaluated against DPWH regional indices and CMPD rates. |
| **8** | **Proof of Land Ownership / ROW Clearances** | Pre-Procurement | Deed of Absolute Sale, Deed of Donation, Writ of Execution, or Sanggunian ROW waiver. |
| **9** | **Environmental Compliance (ECC or CNC)** | Pre-Procurement | Issued by DENR-EMB prior to civil works bidding. |
| **10** | **Water Permit or Application** | Pre-Procurement | Mandatory for water supply projects (NWRB / LWUA). |
| **11** | **Permit to Cut Trees** | Pre-Procurement | Issued by DENR-CENRO/PENRO *(conditional; required only when clearing trees)*. |
| **12** | **NHCP Certification** | Pre-Procurement | National Historical Commission clearance *(conditional; for heritage or historical zones)*. |
| **13** | **Certification on Capability to Implement by Admin** | Pre-Procurement | Issued by DPWH *(conditional; mandatory only if implemented by Administration)*. |
| **14** | **Notice of Award (NOA)** | Procurement | Formal bid award notice under RA 9184 Sec. 37. |
| **15** | **Notice to Proceed (NTP)** | Procurement | Executive issuance authorizing construction commencement; establishes contract start date. |
| **16** | **Contract Agreement** | Procurement | Notarized contract between LGU and winning contractor, including PCAB license and performance security. |
| **17** | **Proof of Project Signboard** | Implementation | Installed billboard conforming strictly to COA Circular No. 2013-004 specifications. |
| **18** | **Request for Work Inspection** | Implementation | Routine contractor requests for engineering phase validation *(conditional)*. |
| **19** | **Concrete Pouring Permit** | Implementation | Mandatory engineering permit issued before structural slab/column pours *(conditional)*. |
| **20** | **Site Instruction** | Implementation | Field log of directives issued by LGU/PDMU supervising engineers *(conditional)*. |
| **21** | **Water Source Validation Certification** | Implementation | Certified by Municipal Engineer for potable water supply systems. |
| **22** | **Notice of Defects and Deficiencies (Annex E)** | Implementation | Official matrix issued upon discovering on-site or documentary non-conformities. |
| **23** | **Variation Orders / Change Orders** | Implementation | Detailed justification and revised plans for additive/deductive works (capped at 10% under RA 9184). |
| **24** | **Statement of Work Accomplished (SWA - Annex F)** | Implementation | Monthly cumulative progress report certified by Municipal Engineer with billing sheets. |
| **25** | **Work Suspension / Resume Orders** | Implementation | Formal orders halting or resuming works due to weather or force majeure *(conditional)*. |
| **26** | **Certificate of Completion (Annex G)** | Closeout | Issued upon 100% physical completion, initiating punchlisting and 1-year DLP. |
| **27** | **Report on Fund Utilization & Status (Annex I)** | Implementation | Monthly/quarterly financial report certified by Accountant and Treasurer. |
| **28** | **Inspection Reports & QAAR Tools** | Implementation | Quality Assurance Assessment Reports and on-site Aide Memoire notes. |
| **29** | **Warranty Security** | Closeout | Bank guarantee or surety bond posted by contractor to guarantee civil works during DLP. |
| **30** | **Progress Photos & Test Results** | Implementation | Geotagged monthly photos and laboratory test results (concrete compression, slump, compaction). |
| **31** | **Construction Schedule (Bar Chart / S-Curve)** | Implementation | Target vs. actual timeline tracking physical slippage. |
| **32** | **Certificate of Final Acceptance** | Closeout | Issued after the 1-year Defect Liability Period (DLP) and post-DLP joint assessment. |
| **33** | **Project Completion Report (Annex J) & Liquidations** | Closeout | Final closeout narrative package, Rapid SubayBAYAN Assessment (RSSA), and SORD (Annex H). |

### 4.2 Motor Vehicle Acquisition (8 Discrete Items)
For rescue vehicles, ambulances, multi-cabs, and heavy equipment:
1. **Notice of Authority to Debit Account Issued (NADAI)**.
2. **Confirmation on Receipt of Fund**.
3. **Proof of Transfer of Fund to LGU Trust Fund**.
4. **Authority to Purchase Motorized Vehicle (APMV)**: Confirmation letter issued by DBM or DILG, enforcing engine displacement limits under DBM BC No. 2022-1 and AO No. 14 s. 2018.
5. **Certificate of Acceptance**: Formal acceptance of delivered units by LGU inspection committee.
6. **Project Profile for Motor Vehicles (New Vehicle)**: Detailed specifications, engine/chassis numbers, and color markings.
7. **Report of Disbursement / Liquidations (Annex H)**.
8. **Report on Fund Utilization and Status of Program/Project Implementation (Annex I)**.

---

### 4.3 Non-Infrastructure & Social Assistance Projects
For medical equipment, educational aid, burial, food, livelihood, and emergency cash assistance:
1. **Notice of Authority to Debit Account Issued (NADAI)**.
2. **Confirmation on Receipt of Fund**.
3. **Proof of Transfer of Fund to LGU Trust Fund**.
4. **Non-Infrastructure Monitoring Templates (Appendices 1–19)**:
   * **MT1, MT1A, MT1B:** Summary, description, and beneficiary distribution receipts for learning materials.
   * **MT2, MT2A–MT2F:** Financial assistance distribution summaries (Medical, Burial, Transportation, Food, Other, Educational).
   * **MT3, MT3A–MT3D:** Livelihood assistance (Cash-for-Work, Individual, Organized Group, Skills Training).
   * **MT4, MT4A:** Medical devices distribution summaries and receipts.
5. **Report of Disbursement / Liquidations (Annex H)**.
6. **Report on Fund Utilization and Status of Implementation (Annex I)**.

## 5. Review Cadence & System Integration

* **Intake & Verification Routing:**
  $$\text{LGU Submission} \longrightarrow \text{Field Officer (C/MLGOO)} \longrightarrow \text{Cluster Head} \longrightarrow \text{PDMU PO Engineer} \longrightarrow \text{Regional Office}$$
* **System Status Lifecycle:**
  * When seeded in `{app_name}`, each required document begins in `compliance_status = "pending"` and `workflow_status = "draft"`.
  * Upon file upload via DMS, `workflow_status` transitions to `"submitted"`, and `compliance_status` enters `"under_review"`.
  * During engineering evaluation, `workflow_status` transitions to `"under_verification"`.
  * If deficiencies are identified, the reviewer returns the document with deficiency remarks: `workflow_status` becomes `"returned_for_revision"`, while `compliance_status` **strictly remains `"under_review"`**.
  * Full compliance (`compliance_status = "compliant"`, `workflow_status = "approved"`) is unlocked only when all technical criteria in Annex C are cleared.
* **Regional Handoff Prerequisite:** A project must achieve 100% compliant status on all applicable Annex B requirements before {app_name} unlocks regional export packaging.
