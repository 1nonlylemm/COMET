# Appendix 5: Report on Beneficiaries Provided with Financial Assistance — Medical (Monitoring Template #2A / MT-2A)

**Appendix 5 (MT-2A)** is the official DILG granular beneficiary tracking roster for medical aid prescribed under the **Omnibus Guidelines** and referenced in the **PDMU Operations Manual** (Step 3 p. 17; Step 4 p. 19; Annex B Table 7). It serves as the primary subordinate attachment to **[Appendix 4 (MT-2)](file:///c:/Users/marka/Projects/COMET/backend/app/brain/reference/appendix_04_mt2_financial_assistance.md)**, itemizing individual patient demographics, representative interview details, attending medical institutions, payment amounts, and verified medical documentation for every recipient of government-funded medical financial assistance.

### 3.1 Section 1: Details of the Patient (Rows 15–17, Columns B–F)
* **`NAME` (Column B):** Full legal name of the patient receiving medical treatment.
* **`SEX` (Column C):** Biological sex of the patient.
* **`AGE` (Column D):** Age of the patient.
* **`ADDRESS` (Column E):** Physical residential address of the patient.
* **`CONTACT NUMBER` (Column F):** Active contact number of the patient or household.

### 3.2 Section 2: Details of Person Interviewed (Rows 15–17, Columns G–J)
Accommodates situations where the patient is incapacitated, hospitalized, a minor, or represented by a relative during social intake:
* **`NAME` (Column G):** Full legal name of the interviewee/claimant.
* **`RELATIONSHIP TO THE PATIENT` (Column H):** Relationship of representative (e.g., *Spouse*, *Mother*, *Father*, *Child*, *Sibling*, *Guardian*).
* **`VALID ID PRESENTED*` (Columns I–J):**
  * **`ID NUMBER` (Column I):** Identification card serial number.
  * **`TYPE` (Column J):** ID category (e.g., PhilSys National ID, UMID, Driver's License, Voter's ID, Senior Citizen ID).
  * *Footnote 1 Rule:* Cell A29 explicitly states: `*If No Valid ID, Barangay Certification will suffice`.

### 3.3 Section 3: Details of Payment (Rows 15–17, Columns K–M)
* **`NAME OF MEDICAL INSTITUTION` (Column K):** Hospital, clinic, diagnostic center, or health facility where medical service is rendered.
* **`DATE RECEIVED` (Column L):** Date the financial assistance was received by the claimant.
* **`AMOUNT RECEIVED` (Column M):** Actual cash grant or guarantee letter amount disbursed ($\text{PHP}$).
* **`TOTAL` (Row 28):** Aggregate financial sum (Columns A–L merged; Column M sum).

### 3.4 Section 4: Supporting Documents Submitted Checkbox Matrix (Columns N–R)
Under Row 30 (`** Put (X) on the supporting documents submitted`), social workers mark `(X)` against five specific documentary prerequisites:
1. **`Case Study Report of P/C/MSWDO ` (Column N):** Social Case Study Report prepared by the LGU Social Welfare and Development Office.
2. **`Medical Certificate or Medical Abstract issued within 3 months` (Column O):** Valid medical assessment issued by the attending physician within 90 days.
3. **`For Hospital Payment- Outstanding Hospital Bill/ Statement of Account ` (Column P):** Current billing statement for in-patient care.
4. **`For medical/assistive devices – Medical Prescriptions ` (Column Q):** Doctor's prescription specifying needed medical or mobility equipment.
5. **`For Medical Procedures – Laboratory Requests ` (Column R):** Attending physician's formal request for diagnostic or clinical procedures.

Appendix 5 features two explicit regulatory instructions printed directly below the matrix:

* **Footnote 1 (Cell A29):**  
  > `*If No Valid ID, Barangay Certification will suffice`  
  *Operational Rule:* Accommodates indigent claimants lacking government-issued identification cards by accepting a formal certification of residency and identity issued by the Punong Barangay.
* **Footnote 2 (Cell A30):**  
  > `** Put (X) on the supporting documents submitted`  
  *Operational Rule:* Mandates marking an `(X)` under Columns N through R to document the specific evidentiary attachments physically verified on file prior to disbursement.

## 5. Four-Tier Signatory Chain

Rows 32–35 define the four-level institutional review and approval hierarchy:

```
PREPARED BY:         REVIEWED BY:         NOTED BY:            APPROVED BY:

(NAME & SIGNATURE)   (NAME & SIGNATURE)   (NAME & SIGNATURE)   (NAME & SIGNATURE)
SOCIAL WORKER         P/C/MSWDO HEAD       P/C/M ACCOUNTANT    LOCAL CHIEF EXECUTIVE
```

1. **`PREPARED BY:` Social Worker (Cell A35: `SOCIAL WORKER`):**
   * The examining Social Worker who interviews the claimant, assesses eligibility, verifies medical documents, and compiles the roster.
2. **`REVIEWED BY:` SWDO Head (Cell G35: ` P/C/MSWDO HEAD`):**
   * The Provincial, City, or Municipal Social Welfare and Development Officer Head (noted: source label contains a leading space: ` P/C/MSWDO HEAD`) who reviews the casework and programmatic compliance.
3. **`NOTED BY:` Accountant (Cell K35: ` P/C/M ACCOUNTANT`):**
   * The Provincial, City, or Municipal Accountant (noted: source label contains a leading space: ` P/C/M ACCOUNTANT`) who notes the financial disbursement against LGU books.
4. **`APPROVED BY:` Local Chief Executive (Cell P35: `LOCAL CHIEF EXECUTIVE`):**
   * The Municipal Mayor, City Mayor, or Provincial Governor who authorizes the medical assistance disbursement on behalf of the implementing LGU.

## 6. Official Policy Guidelines and Allowable Assistance Caps

Rows 37–45 of the primary template print official program definitions and statutory monetary thresholds:

### 6.1 Program Scope Definition (Row 37)
> *"MEDICAL ASSISTANCE is the assistance to help shoulder hospitalization expenses, cost of medicines, other medical treatment or procedures such as implants, laboratory procedures including but not limited to computerized tomography (CT) scan, electrocardiogram (ECG), echocardiogram (2D Echo), magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) and provision of assistive device. Other medical expenses such as professional fees may be covered. Birthing is not covered, unless the patient suffered from complications during delivery, subject to the assessment of the social worker. The client shall not be entitled to in-patient financial assistance except for instances when the drug or treatment is not available in the hospital or covered by the Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth)."*

### 6.2 Allowable Assistance Amounts and Cadences (Rows 42–45)
The template prints three explicit monetary ceilings and frequency limits:

| Assistance Sub-Category | Allowable Monetary Range | Frequency Constraint | Source Reference |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **(1) Hospitalization or hospital bill** | $\text{PHP } 1,000 \text{ to } \text{PHP } 150,000$ | Once every three (3) months | Row 43 |
| **(2) Medicines (out-patient)** | $\text{PHP } 1,000 \text{ to } \text{PHP } 10,000$ | Once every three (3) months | Row 44 |
| **(3) Laboratory Procedures (out-patient)** | $\text{PHP } 1,000 \text{ to } \text{PHP } 10,000$ | Once every three (3) months | Row 45 |

## 7. Practical Questions the Smart Assistant Should Answer

* **What is Appendix 5 / MT-2A?**  
  *Answer:* Appendix 5 (Monitoring Template 2A or MT-2A) is the official DILG Report on Beneficiaries Provided with Financial Assistance — Sub-Type: Medical Financial Assistance. It is an 18-column beneficiary roster used by LGUs to record individual patient details, representative information, payment amounts, and verified medical supporting documents.
* **What are the allowable financial caps for medical assistance in Appendix 5?**  
  *Answer:* As printed on the face of the form: (1) Hospitalization ranges from $\text{PHP } 1,000$ to $\text{PHP } 150,000$ once every three months; (2) Out-patient medicines range from $\text{PHP } 1,000$ to $\text{PHP } 10,000$ once every three months; and (3) Out-patient laboratory procedures range from $\text{PHP } 1,000$ to $\text{PHP } 10,000$ once every three months.
* **What identification is required on MT-2A if a claimant lacks a valid ID?**  
  *Answer:* Footnote 1 explicitly states: *"If No Valid ID, Barangay Certification will suffice."*
* **What supporting documents must be marked on MT-2A?**  
  *Answer:* Under Footnote 2, social workers mark `(X)` across five categories: (1) Case Study Report of P/C/MSWDO; (2) Medical Certificate or Abstract issued within 3 months; (3) Outstanding Hospital Bill / Statement of Account; (4) Medical Prescriptions (for medicines/assistive devices); and (5) Laboratory Requests (for medical procedures).
* **Who signs Appendix 5 (MT-2A)?**  
  *Answer:* MT-2A is signed across four tiers: prepared by the **Social Worker**, reviewed by the **P/C/MSWDO Head**, noted by the **P/C/M Accountant**, and approved by the **Local Chief Executive**.
