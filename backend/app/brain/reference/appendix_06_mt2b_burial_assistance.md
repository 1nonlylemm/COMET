# Appendix 6: Report on Beneficiaries Provided with Financial Assistance — Burial (Monitoring Template #2B / MT-2B)

**Appendix 6 (MT-2B)** is the official DILG granular beneficiary tracking roster for funeral and burial aid prescribed under the **Omnibus Guidelines** and referenced in the **PDMU Operations Manual** (Step 3 p. 17; Step 4 p. 19; Annex B Table 7). It serves as the primary subordinate attachment to **[Appendix 4 (MT-2)](file:///c:/Users/marka/Projects/COMET/backend/app/brain/reference/appendix_04_mt2_financial_assistance.md)**, itemizing deceased demographic details, ethnicity, cause of death, claimant/interviewee identity, disbursement amounts, and verified civil registry and funeral documentation for every recipient of government-funded burial financial assistance.

### 3.1 Section 1: Details of the Deceased (Rows 15–17, Columns B–G)
* **`NAME` (Column B):** Full legal name of the deceased individual.
* **`SEX` (Column C):** Biological sex of the deceased.
* **`AGE` (Column D):** Age of the deceased at the time of death.
* **`ETHNICITY` (Column E):** Ethnic group or cultural affiliation (relevant for customary burial practices, particularly for Indigenous Peoples).
* **`CAUSE OF DEATH` (Column F):** Official cause of mortality as stated in medical or death registry records.
* **`DATE OF DEATH` (Column G):** Formal date of demise.

### 3.2 Section 2: Details of Person Interviewed (Rows 15–17, Columns H–M)
Captures the surviving family member or authorized representative who filed the claim:
* **`NAME` (Column H):** Full legal name of the claimant/interviewee.
* **`ADDRESS ` (Column I):** Residential address of the claimant (noted: source label contains a trailing space: `ADDRESS `).
* **`RELATIONSHIP TO THE DECEASED` (Column J):** Kinship or relationship (e.g., *Surviving Spouse*, *Parent*, *Child*, *Sibling*).
* **`CONTACT NUMBER` (Column K):** Active telephone or mobile number.
* **`VALID ID PRESENTED*` (Columns L–M):**
  * **`ID NUMBER` (Column L):** Identification card serial number.
  * **`TYPE` (Column M):** ID category (e.g., PhilSys National ID, UMID, Driver's License, Voter's ID).
  * *Footnote 1 Rule:* Cell A29 explicitly states: `*If No Valid ID, Barangay Certification will suffice`.

### 3.3 Section 3: Details of Payment (Rows 15–17, Columns N–O)
* **`DATE RECEIVED` (Column N):** Date the financial assistance was disbursed to the claimant.
* **`AMOUNT RECEIVED` (Column O):** Actual monetary amount disbursed ($\text{PHP}$).
* **`TOTAL` (Row 28):** Aggregate financial sum (Columns A–N merged; Column O sum).

### 3.4 Section 4: Supporting Documents Submitted Checkbox Matrix (Columns P–R)
Under Row 30 (`** Put (X) on the supporting documents submitted`), social workers mark `(X)` against three specific evidentiary attachments:
1. **`Death Certificate or any Applicable Form of Certification ` (Column P):** Registered Certificate of Death or tribal chieftain certification for Indigenous Cultural Communities.
2. **`For Regular Burial Assistance - Funeral Contract\n` (Column Q):** Formal contract or official receipt from the providing funeral parlor/service (noted: source label contains a newline: `Funeral Contract\n`).
3. **`For Transfer of Deceased -Transfer Permit ` (Column R):** Official local health authority transfer permit authorizing the transport of cadavers or human remains across municipal or provincial borders.

## 4. Mandatory Footnotes and Evidentiary Rules

Appendix 6 features two explicit regulatory instructions printed directly below the matrix:

* **Footnote 1 (Cell A29):**  
  > `*If No Valid ID, Barangay Certification will suffice`  
  *Operational Rule:* Accommodates indigent claimants lacking formal government-issued identity cards by accepting a certification of residency and relationship issued by the Punong Barangay.
* **Footnote 2 (Cell A30):**  
  > `** Put (X) on the supporting documents submitted`  
  *Operational Rule:* Mandates marking an `(X)` under Columns P, Q, and R to verify that the required death certificate, funeral contract, or transfer permit is physically on file before release of public funds.

## 5. Four-Tier Signatory Chain

Rows 32–35 define the four-level institutional review and approval hierarchy:

```
PREPARED BY:         REVIEWED BY:         NOTED BY:            APPROVED BY:

(NAME & SIGNATURE)   (NAME & SIGNATURE)   (NAME & SIGNATURE)   (NAME & SIGNATURE)
SOCIAL WORKER         P/C/MSWDO HEAD       P/C/M ACCOUNTANT    LOCAL CHIEF EXECUTIVE
```

1. **`PREPARED BY:` Social Worker (Cell A35: `SOCIAL WORKER`):**
   * The examining Social Worker who interviews the claimant, validates death certificates and funeral contracts, and compiles the roster.
2. **`REVIEWED BY:` SWDO Head (Cell F35: ` P/C/MSWDO HEAD`):**
   * The Provincial, City, or Municipal Social Welfare and Development Officer Head (noted: source label contains a leading space: ` P/C/MSWDO HEAD`) who reviews casework and program eligibility.
3. **`NOTED BY:` Accountant (Cell K35: ` P/C/M ACCOUNTANT`):**
   * The Provincial, City, or Municipal Accountant (noted: source label contains a leading space: ` P/C/M ACCOUNTANT`) who notes the disbursement in LGU books.
4. **`APPROVED BY:` Local Chief Executive (Cell P35: `LOCAL CHIEF EXECUTIVE`):**
   * The Municipal Mayor, City Mayor, or Provincial Governor who authorizes the burial assistance disbursement on behalf of the implementing LGU.

## 6. Official Policy Guidelines and Allowable Assistance Caps

Rows 37–44 of the primary template print official program definitions and statutory monetary thresholds:

### 6.1 Program Scope Definition (Row 37)
> *"BURIAL ASSISTANCE is the assistance to defray funeral and related expenses, including but not limited to expenses in bringing the remains to the residence of the deceased and/or burial site in accordance with existing customary practices of the family especially among Indigenous Peoples. - Due to a disaster/calamity/critical events or similar circumstances where there are casualties in the family, the surviving family shall be given an outright cash in the amount of Php10,000.00 for each casualty, without need of a case study report."*

### 6.2 Allowable Assistance Amounts and Special Circumstances (Rows 41–44)
The template prints three explicit monetary schedules:

| Assistance Sub-Category | Allowable Monetary Range | Source Conditions / Provisions | Source Reference |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :---: |
| **(1) Funeral Expenses** | $\text{PHP } 5,000 \text{ to } 25,000$ | Standard funeral and casket expenses (noted: source prints `P5,000 - 25,000` without 'P' on ceiling). | Row 42 |
| **(2) Transfer of Cadaver** | $\text{PHP } 5,000 \text{ to } \text{PHP } 25,000$ | Cost of transporting human remains; note allows one client to avail of both funeral and transfer of cadaver simultaneously. | Row 43 |
| **(3) Casualties during disaster/calamity** | $\text{PHP } 10,000 \text{ per casualty}$ | Outright cash grant per deceased family member resulting from disaster/critical event; exempt from case study report requirement. | Row 44 |

## 7. Practical Questions the Smart Assistant Should Answer

* **What is Appendix 6 / MT-2B?**  
  *Answer:* Appendix 6 (Monitoring Template 2B or MT-2B) is the official DILG Report on Beneficiaries Provided with Financial Assistance — Sub-Type: Burial Financial Assistance. It is an 18-column beneficiary roster used by LGUs to record deceased demographics, ethnicity, cause of death, claimant representative details, payment amounts, and verified funeral/death records.
* **What are the allowable financial caps for burial assistance in Appendix 6?**  
  *Answer:* As printed on the face of the form: (1) Funeral expenses range from $\text{PHP } 5,000$ to $\text{PHP } 25,000$; (2) Transfer of cadaver ranges from $\text{PHP } 5,000$ to $\text{PHP } 25,000$ (a client may avail of both funeral and transfer services simultaneously); and (3) Disaster/calamity casualties are granted an outright $\text{PHP } 10,000$ per casualty without need of a social case study report.
* **Can a beneficiary receive both funeral assistance and transfer of cadaver assistance?**  
  *Answer:* Yes. Note 2 of the form explicitly provides that *"one client may avail of one or two services at the same time - funeral and transfer of cadaver."*
* **When is a social case study report waived for burial assistance?**  
  *Answer:* Under the official guideline printed in Row 37, when casualties occur due to a disaster, calamity, or critical event, surviving families are provided outright cash of $\text{PHP } 10,000$ per casualty without requiring a case study report.
* **Who signs Appendix 6 (MT-2B)?**  
  *Answer:* MT-2B is signed across four tiers: prepared by the **Social Worker**, reviewed by the **P/C/MSWDO Head**, noted by the **P/C/M Accountant**, and approved by the **Local Chief Executive**.
