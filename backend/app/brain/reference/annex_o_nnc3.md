# Notice of Non-Compliance No. 3 (NNC3 / Annex O)

## 1. Statutory Identification & Authority
* **Official Document Title:** Notice of Non-Compliance No. 3.
* **System Template Convention:** Annex O (internal system code).
* **Authority Level:** Level 1 (PDMU Operations Manual, pp. 18, 21, 26, 51).
* **Authorized Signatory:** **Regional Director (RD)** of DILG Region IV-A (Calabarzon) ONLY.
* **Legal Invariant:** The Provincial Director or PDMU Chief **CANNOT** sign or issue NNC3.

---

## 2. Triggering Conditions & Legal Consequences
* **Trigger:** Issued when non-compliance persists for **15 working days** following official receipt of Notice of Non-Compliance No. 2 (NNC2).
* **Legal & Administrative Ramifications:**
  * Official transmittal of the case file to **DILG Legal Service / Bantay Korapsyon Team** for administrative investigation.
  * Direct endorsement to the Office of the Undersecretary for Local Government (**OUSLG**).
  * Suspension of further fund tranches or project forfeiture proceedings.

---

## 3. System Implementation
* In the system, PDMU staff prepare the NNC3 draft transmittal package along with the complete audit history.
* The system prevents designation of local provincial officers as final signatories on NNC3, adhering strictly to regional authority rules.
