# Annex K: Sample Letter of Commitment

---

## 1. Verified Document Identification & Purpose

* **Primary Source Document:** `others/reference_data/COMET Files, Forms, Annexes, and More!/Omnibus Guidelines Forms Annexes/Annex K - Sample Letter of Commitment.docx`
* **Official Document Header:** `[SAMPLE] Letter of Commitment`
* **Formal Document Heading:** `LETTER OF COMMITMENT`
* **Authority Level:** **Level 1 (Official Authoritative Policy)**
* **Document Nature:** An executive written commitment / pledge executed by the **Local Chief Executive (Mayor or Governor)**. While the signatory block styles the LCE as **"Affiant"** and uses the recitation "hereby commit and state", the form itself is titled `LETTER OF COMMITMENT` and does **not** contain an oath, jurat, or notarial acknowledgment block.
* **Intended Purpose:** Serves as an executive commitment instrument executed by an implementing Local Government Unit (LGU) when a nationally funded project remains ongoing near the expiration of its fund validity period. Under this instrument, the LGU formally pledges to:
  1. Revert unutilized grant funds to the National Treasury (Bureau of the Treasury / BTr);
  2. Appropriate and allocate counterpart local government funds equivalent to the reverted amount to complete remaining civil works;
  3. Adhere to a bilaterally agreed completion deadline established with the DILG Regional Office (RO); and
  4. Submit the Project Completion Report (PCR / Annex J) within thirty (30) calendar days following the issuance of the Certificate of Completion.

## 3. The Three (3) Codified Source Undertakings

The substantive body of Annex K consists of three (3) explicit, non-severable representations executed by the Local Chief Executive:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        ANNEX K: THREE CODIFIED UNDERTAKINGS                            │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. FUND REVERSION & LOCAL FUND APPROPRIATION                                           │
│    "THAT, the (name of Local Government Unit (LGU)) shall revert the unutilized funds  │
│    to the Bureau of the Treasury (BTr) and allocate local funds equivalent to the      │
│    reverted amount for the remaining works;"                                           │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. MANDATORY COMPLETION DEADLINE                                                       │
│    "THAT, the (name of LGU) shall complete the project not later than                  │
│    (agreed time with DILG RO);"                                                        │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. PROJECT COMPLETION REPORT (PCR) SUBMISSION WINDOW                                   │
│    "THAT, the (name of LGU) shall submit a Project Completion Report (PCR) within      │
│    thirty (30) calendar days to DILG-RO through the DILG Provincial Office after the    │
│    issuance of the Certificate of Completion."                                         │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Substantive Analysis of Undertakings

1. **Reversion and Local Funding Replacement:**
   * Grounded in the **One-Year Cash Budgeting System** under the annual General Appropriations Act (GAA) and DBM Local Budget Circulars (e.g., LBC 155).
   * Prevents projects from stalling when national fund validity expires by committing the LGU to assume financial responsibility using its own local development funds.
2. **Bilateral Target Completion Date:**
   * The completion date is not unilaterally imposed; it is formally negotiated and agreed with the **DILG Regional Office (RO)** based on verified remaining physical works.
3. **PCR Submission Timeline:**
   * Aligns with **Operations Manual p. 26** and **Annex J**, fixing the closeout submission deadline at **thirty (30) calendar days** after the Local Chief Executive issues the Certificate of Completion (Annex G).

## 5. Program Lifecycle & Procedural Timing (PDMU Manual p. 20)

Under the **PDMU Operations Manual Final (p. 20, Step No. 4, Sequence 4)**, the execution of Annex K is embedded into the formal project closeout and validity expiration lifecycle:

* **Triggering Event:** Projects that are **still ongoing near the end of their statutory fund validity period**.
* **Responsible Initiating Party:** **DILG Regional Office (RO)**.
* **Issuance Window:** Formally initiated during the **last month of the fund validity period**.
* **Procedural Action:** DILG RO issues formal transmittal letters directing LGUs with uncompleted works to commit to project completion using local funds and execute the **Letter of Commitment (Annex K)**.
* **Condition of Applicability:** Explicitly noted in the Operations Manual as:
  > *"Applicable to LGUs who cannot transfer the LGSF to trust accounts."*

### Lifecycle Contrast: Standard Closeout vs. Fund Validity Expiration

```
STANDARD CLOSEOUT (Normal Completion):
100% Civil Works -> Final SWA (Annex F) -> Joint Inspection -> Certificate of Completion (Annex G)
  -> 30-Day Window: PCR (Annex J) Due
  -> COA-Stamped Annex H / SORD Liquidated
  -> If Balance > 0: Reversion within 3 months of Final Billing

FUND VALIDITY EXPIRATION DURING ACTIVE EXECUTION (Delayed Completion):
Project Still Ongoing Near Expiration of Fund Validity Period
  -> Last Month of Fund Validity: DILG RO Issues Directive
  -> LCE Executes Annex K (Letter of Commitment) as Affiant:
       (a) Reverts unutilized LGSF funds to BTr
       (b) Allocates equal local counterpart funds to complete civil works
       (c) Pledges completion by agreed DILG RO target date
       (d) Commits to 30-calendar-day PCR submission post-Annex G
```

## 6. Evidentiary Boundaries & System Implementation

### What the Source Explicitly Establishes
1. The document designates the **Local Chief Executive (Mayor/Governor)** as the signatory, styled on the signature line as **Affiant**.
2. It requires both fund reversion to BTr and local counterpart budget appropriation.
3. It binds the LGU to an agreed completion target date and a 30-day PCR deadline following Certificate of Completion.

### What the Source Does NOT Establish
1. **Not a Notarized Affidavit:** The template contains no jurat ("Subscribed and sworn to before me..."), notarial seal, or administering officer block; it is an administrative letter of commitment rather than a sworn legal affidavit.
2. **No Automatic Penalties on the Form:** The form itself does not specify immediate fines or criminal sanctions; failure to fulfill Annex K commitments escalates through the administrative notice framework (Reminder $\rightarrow$ NNC1 $\rightarrow$ NNC2 $\rightarrow$ NNC3 $\rightarrow$ Bantay Korapsyon / OUSLG).
3. **Not Mandatory for Completed Projects:** Annex K is not required for projects that reach 100% completion within their normal fund validity window.
4. **No Automatic System Workflow Mutation:** In {app_name}, Annex K does not auto-generate via a single button; it is tracked as a specialized compliance/closeout document requirement when fund reversion and local funding assumptions are recorded.
